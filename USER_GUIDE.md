# Mayor WhatsApp Poster — Quick Start & User Guide

Welcome to **Mayor WhatsApp Poster**! This guide is designed to help you set up your account and schedule your first WhatsApp campaign in under 5 minutes.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Link your WhatsApp Account
To start sending messages, you need to connect your WhatsApp account to our secure backend gateway.

1. Navigate to the **WhatsApp Status** page from the sidebar menu.
2. Click the **`+ Add Account`** button.
3. A QR code will display on your screen.
4. Open the WhatsApp app on your mobile phone:
   * **Android**: Tap the three vertical dots (menu) in the top-right corner $\rightarrow$ **Linked devices** $\rightarrow$ **Link a device**.
   * **iPhone**: Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Linked Devices** $\rightarrow$ **Link a Device**.
5. Scan the QR code displayed on your screen.
6. Once scanned, your session status will update to **`CONNECTED`**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **To keep your connection active**:
> WhatsApp requires you to open the WhatsApp app on your primary mobile phone and connect to the internet at least once every 14 days. If your phone remains offline or inactive, WhatsApp's servers will automatically disconnect your session for security.

---

## 👥 Step 2: Create an Audience List
An **Audience List** is a saved collection of WhatsApp groups. Instead of manually selecting groups every time you post, you can group them under an audience list (e.g., "Real Estate Groups", "Daily Updates").

1. Navigate to the **Audience Lists** page from the sidebar menu.
2. Click **`+ Add Audience List`**.
3. Enter a descriptive name for your group collection.
4. Search or select the target WhatsApp groups from your linked account that you want to include in this collection.
5. Click **`Save Audience List`**.

---

## 📣 Step 3: Schedule and Post Announcements
Once your WhatsApp is connected and your Audience List is ready, you can schedule your announcements.

1. Navigate to the **Announcements** page and click **`+ New Announcement`**.
2. **Sender Session**: Select which linked WhatsApp number you want to send the message from.
3. **Target Groups**: Choose the **Audience List** you created in Step 2.
4. **Announcements Title**: Give your campaign a internal reference name.
5. **Media Attachment (Optional)**: Upload one or more images or videos. If you upload multiple media files, the app's *Dynamic Media Rotator* will automatically cycle through them on subsequent posts.
6. **Caption and Variations**:
   * Type your message caption.
   * **Pro-Tip**: Click **`+ Add Variation`** to add alternative ways to write the message. The system will rotate through these variations to keep your posts unique and prevent your phone number from getting flagged by WhatsApp's spam filters.
7. **Schedule Settings**:
   * **One-time**: Set a date and time for the message to fire once.
   * **Recurring**: Select the recurrence interval (e.g., *Every 2 days*) or specific *Days of the Week* (e.g., Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays) and set the post time.
8. Click **`Schedule Announcement`** to save.

---

## 🛡️ Anti-Spam Best Practices (Avoid Bans)
To keep your WhatsApp account safe from suspensions, follow these guidelines:

* **Use Spin-Tax / Variations**: Always use at least 2 or 3 caption variations for recurring campaigns.
* **Keep your primary phone online**: If your phone goes offline, WhatsApp may flag your bot session.
* **Respect Spam Limits**: Avoid scheduling posts to run too frequently. A spacing of at least 6 to 12 hours between recurring announcements is recommended.
* **Set Send Delays**: In the settings tab, make sure your send delay is configured (we recommend at least 5 to 10 seconds between group messages to mimic natural human behavior).
